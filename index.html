About

Behind the Decks

Joe reads a room like a musician, because most nights he is one — when he's not open-format DJing weddings and parties around the Valley, he's fronting the Arizona indie rock band Alibi. That background shapes the sets: song selection and transitions that actually track the night's energy, from a quiet dinner hour to a room that won't leave the floor at midnight. Tell him the vibe and the must-plays (and the do-not-plays) — he'll handle the rest, mic included.
